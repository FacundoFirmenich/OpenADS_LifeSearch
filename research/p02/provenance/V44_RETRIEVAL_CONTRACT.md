# v4.4 recovery comparisons — before inspection of new outcomes

The frozen archive and results remain the primary historical evidence. Compare
the historical and broad symmetric query on the **same** live SciX session,
using the same year, article, field, sort and pagination options. Then compare
both live sets to the archived sets. These are differences between returned
sets, not precision or recall. Index evolution, historic export policy and
identifier changes cannot be disentangled by the archive contrast alone.
Resolve the 15 anchor DOIs/bibcodes separately by identifier on the same live
endpoint, before labeling an omission as not found by the two queries. Do
not change templates after results. Record endpoint, UTC, exact query,
pagination, declared counts, checksum and elapsed observation interval.

## Reference-frame pilot independent of the ADS archive

Use the publisher-deposited reference lists for Hendrix et al. (2019; ocean
worlds), Cockell et al. (2016; habitability), and Neveu et al. (2018;
life detection), pre-existing citations in P01/P02. Use Crossref DOI-linked
references from all three, as deposited, not a new search over P02. Capture
unresolved and DOI-free reference slots in provenance; preserve source
memberships and DOI identity. Retain candidate journal articles in
2000--2023 for blinded coding. Do not use title target matches as eligibility
or discard articles with no named target in the title. Label by
paper-target: primary research with a materially relevant local environment,
physical/chemical habitability constraint, biosignature test, or sample-access
measurement. A review, mission concept only, or passing mention does not count.
Separate procedure from claim validity; disputed primary analyses and direct
counter-analyses may qualify. Two independent coders assign each source
candidate, blinded to archive hits and citation counts. Preserve uncertainty
and disagreements; adjudicate with evidence pointers before unblinding.

This reference frame may be small or incomplete for individual targets. It
does not enumerate all eligible literature. A complete census of the eligible
records gives exact coverage for that documented frame, not a binomial
confidence interval for a larger universe; a probability sample would need
design-based uncertainty. Treat any local title join as a provisional
matching aid requiring checked DOI/bibcode identity, not as a primary result.
Never use the 720 archive-sampled records as a recall denominator. Those
separate records support design-weighted precision after independent labels.
