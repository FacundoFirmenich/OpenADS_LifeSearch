# Measurement graph and admissible claims

`scientific target → ADS query/retrieval → paper–target assignment → publication-
year completeness gate → optional target split → optional team-size transform →
time/citation/geographic transform → estimate → pairwise identification status
or partial order`

Every arrow is a potential measurement decision and is represented in the
specification multiverse where the archived fields permit it.

Evidence levels must never be collapsed:

`publication affiliation < agency participation < mission concept < approved
mission < funded and scheduled mission`.

The primary estimands are descriptive properties of the recovered literature.
They do not estimate scientific quality, habitability, the probability of life,
national capability, labour productivity, or the normative priority of a
mission.

Two citation reference graphs are retained: pooled same-year normalization and
leave-one-target-out same-year normalization. Agreement is a robustness result;
neither graph supplies field normalization. The Enceladus title/keyword screen
is a triage graph with explicit unresolved states, not a precision/recall audit.
The Pareto graph is calibrated against an independent-continuous-rank null so
that a large frontier is not mistaken for an exceptional empirical pattern.
