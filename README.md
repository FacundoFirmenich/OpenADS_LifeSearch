# OpenADS LifeSearch

OpenADS LifeSearch hosts the reproducibility materials for P02 v4.5, a
retrospective study of planetary habitability evidence and research attention
across Enceladus, Europa, Ganymede, Mars, Titan and Venus.

- [P02 methods, data access and replay](research/p02/README.md)
- [Epistemic graph](research/p02/companion/epistemic_graph.html) and
  [Supergraph](research/p02/companion/supergraph.html). Download both HTML
  files into the same directory and open either one locally.
- [Contrast estimates and simultaneous intervals](research/p02/results/joint_trend_contrasts.csv)
- [Affiliation and agency findings for Enceladus](research/p02/provenance/ENCELADUS_AFFILIATIONS.md)
- [Software citation](CITATION.cff)

The historical archive has 10,317 target–paper assignments and 9,513 unique
bibcodes. The 2000–2023 complete-year study uses 9,762 assignments and 9,021
unique papers. In that study, 16 pair–window–estimator directions receive
joint support under two paper-resampling schemes. The eight descriptive
reversals do not receive joint support in both directions. The archive
misses two independently identified studies in a purposive panel of 15.
Those two cases document specific omissions and supply no recall estimate.

The three original top-level Python tools are historical prototypes. Their
FTE, budget-return, Decadal and mission-priority measures are outside P02's
validated results. Use the [claim ledger](research/p02/provenance/claim_ledger.csv)
for the audit of the earlier claims. The legacy ADS tool requires the
operator's own authorized token through an environment variable.
